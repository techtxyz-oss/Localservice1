Gaya district mein kitne blocks hain?

Gaya district ki official website par 24 development blocks list kiye gaye hain. Yahan wahi block names service-area directory ke roop mein diye gaye hain.

Kya har block mein service provider available hai?

Provider ki coverage aur availability badal sakti hai. WhatsApp request bhejne ke baad booking se pehle confirmation lein.

Service ka final price kaise pata chalega?

Card ya listing par dikhayi gayi koi bhi shuruaati keemat final bill nahi hoti. Kaam shuru hone se pehle provider se total price aur extra charges confirm karein.
